# 👋 Hi, I'm Davey Morgan

### Creative Technologist · Multidisciplinary Visual Storyteller · Maker

I’m a **multidisciplinary creative technologist** working across software, physical computing, AI, robotics, digital design, electronics, and fabrication to build **interactive experiences, experimental systems, and functional prototypes**. My technical work includes **HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Python, shell scripting, C#, and C++**, alongside **Raspberry Pi, Linux, ESP32, Arduino, sensors, microcontrollers, electronics, soldering, and embedded hardware**. I work with **AI tools and large language models**, exploring how LLM-driven interfaces, automation, intelligent systems, and emerging technologies can connect with creative and technical applications. My projects often combine code with **3D modeling and fabrication, custom enclosures and components, electronics, and visual design**, allowing ideas to move from software and digital concepts into physical, interactive systems. Alongside this technical work, I bring more than **20 years of multidisciplinary creative experience** across illustration, photography, graphic design, motion, video, writing, and publishing.

## ⚙️ How I Build

My projects usually begin with a problem, interaction, story, or physical idea rather than a specific technology. From there, I prototype across disciplines, choosing whatever combination of **code, electronics, fabrication, visual design, motion, or narrative** best serves the concept. That might mean writing software, configuring Linux on a Raspberry Pi, programming an ESP32, wiring sensors, designing and fabricating an enclosure, building an interface, integrating an LLM, or developing the visual language around the finished system.

I’m especially drawn to projects where the boundaries between **creative practice and engineering** become difficult to separate.

## 🧪 Projects

A few of the systems and prototypes I’m currently developing:

- **ESP32 Home / Car Hub**  
  A compact connected media and control system built around ESP32, exploring Apple Music and other streaming-service integrations for home and automotive environments.

- **Pet Robots for Children**  
  Interactive companion robots that combine physical design, electronics, sensors, software, character, and child-centered interaction.

- **Social Event Friend Match Bracelets**  
  Wearable electronic devices designed to help people discover potential social connections at events through interactive hardware and software.

- **Retro Gaming Console**  
  A custom-built retro-style game system combining electronics, enclosure design, software, and original self-designed games.

- **AI Desk Buddy**  
  A physical desktop companion exploring the intersection of LLMs, interactive software, electronics, and embodied human-computer interaction.
